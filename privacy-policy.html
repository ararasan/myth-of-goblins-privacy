Myth of Goblins – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the mobile game Myth of Goblins (package name com.arara.mythofgoblins, "the game") collects, how it is used and who it is shared with. The game is made by an independent developer ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not run any servers and we do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

Your game progress (profile names you type in, levels, coins, gems, items and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us.

2. Services from other companies

The game uses the following third-party services. They may collect information from your device as described in their own privacy policies.

  • Google Play (in-app purchases). When you buy gems, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google shares with the game only the information needed to confirm the purchase (such as an order ID and the item bought). Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads (optional rewarded ads). If you choose to watch an ad for free gems, Unity Ads may collect device information such as your device's advertising ID, device model, operating system, IP address, language and how you interact with ads. This is used to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity (game engine and in-app purchasing services). The Unity engine and Unity's purchasing services may collect basic technical information (such as device type, operating system and crash information) to make the game work. Unity Privacy Policy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

3. Co-op mode (same Wi-Fi)

Co-op lets two phones on the same Wi-Fi network play together. Your profile name, leader level and game actions are sent directly to the other player's phone on your local network only. Nothing is sent to us or to the internet.

4. How information is used

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To process gem purchases (through Google Play).
  • To show optional rewarded ads (through Unity Ads).

We do not sell your information.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game progress stays on your device until you delete it in the game or uninstall the game. Uninstalling the game removes all of its saved data from your device. Information collected by Google or Unity is kept according to their own policies; you can contact them, or us, to ask about it.

7. Security

Game saves are stored on your device and protected against tampering. Purchases are verified with Google Play's signature before gems are given.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
